# Privacy Policy — VLN Whitelisted

**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

## 1. Introduction

VLN Whitelisted is a Discord application developed for the VALENTINE REDM S7 community.

Our application is integrated with our RedM game server to provide secure whitelist verification and player access control.

We respect user privacy and limit the processing of Discord information to what is necessary for player authorization.

## 2. Information We Process

Our application processes the following information when verifying a player's eligibility to access our RedM server:

- Discord User ID.
- Discord server membership status.
- Discord role information, including the Whitelist role.

This information is retrieved to verify whether a player has permission to join our game server.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use Discord information exclusively to:

- Verify player whitelist status.
- Confirm that a player has the required Discord role.
- Allow authorized players to connect to our RedM server.
- Deny access to players who do not meet whitelist requirements.
- Maintain secure and controlled access to our community.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

VLN Whitelisted performs role verification directly through Discord.

Our whitelist verification system does not maintain a persistent external database of Discord user IDs, member roles, or player verification results.

Information is processed as needed to perform authorization checks and is not retained by the application for unrelated purposes.

## 5. Data Sharing

Discord membership and role information is used for internal whitelist verification between our Discord application and RedM server integration.

We do not sell Discord user information or share it with advertisers.

## 6. Message Content

Our whitelist verification functionality does not require reading or storing the content of users' Discord messages.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect information processed during whitelist verification.

Access to application credentials and server configuration is restricted to authorized administrators.

## 8. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may contact the application administrator to request information about how their Discord data is processed.

Because our whitelist verification system does not persistently store Discord member information outside Discord, there is generally no retained whitelist verification database record to delete.

Users may also contact our administrators regarding their whitelist authorization status.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application's functionality or data processing practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
